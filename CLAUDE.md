# Flywheelify — Homepage Spec

## Overview

A one-page founder transformation funnel designed to convert qualified founders into booking a **Founder Business Audit**. This is NOT a traditional website — it is a cinematic, premium, emotionally intelligent founder experience that guides the visitor from overwhelmed to clarity to action.

**Owner:** Justin  
**Company:** Flywheelify  
**Email:** justin@flywheelify.com  
**Tech Stack:** Next.js  
**Primary CTA:** Book a Founder Business Audit (via embedded GHL survey form)

---

## Target Audience

- Founders doing **$100K–$3M/year** in revenue
- Online businesses: agencies, coaching, consulting, high-ticket services
- Sell a high-ticket offer ($5K–$100K+), usually sold on sales calls
- Often have a sales team, appointment setters, or do sales themselves
- Feel trapped inside their own business despite real growth
- NOT digital nomad language — just "founders"

---

## Brand Positioning

**Tagline:**  
> "Helping Founders build a scalable machine that grows your business, your impact, and your life."

**Core message:**  
Flywheelify helps founders build the operational infrastructure — systems, automation, reporting, delegation — that lets the business grow without depending on the founder for everything.

**Emotional journey the page should create:**  
`Overwhelm → Recognition → Clarity → Relief → Trust → Action`

**What this is NOT:**  
- Not an agency
- Not a consultant
- Not a mastermind
- Not another course

## Social Proof / Credibility Numbers

- Clients have generated over **$2,000,000 in sales**

## Design System

### Emotional Direction

The design should feel:
- **Immersive, cinematic, organic, premium, spacious, calm**
- Like forest air, ocean breeze, warm sunlight
- Like a calm creative workspace — not a dashboard
- Like a luxury founder operating system

It should NOT feel:
- Corporate, startup-y, SaaS-like, sharp, sterile, crowded, robotic, generic, hustle-culture

**Core rule:** Everything should breathe. Whitespace IS the design.

---

### Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Forest Green | `#1F4D3A` | Primary headlines, dark sections, major CTAs, hero emphasis |
| Evergreen | `#2F684F` | Hover states, interactive feedback, secondary CTA accents |
| Sage | `#ABC3A0` | Soft backgrounds, calm accents, muted cards — use sparingly |
| Ocean Teal | `#2FA4A9` | Accent moments, highlighted words — use sparingly |
| Deep Ocean | `#1C3D5A` | Body text, UI labels, navigation, secondary headings |
| Light Cream | `#FAF8F4` | **Primary background** — the "air" of the brand |
| Warm Sand | `#F4F1EA` | Alternate sections, warm cards, layered depth |
| Soft Beige | `#E8E2D8` | Borders, dividers, muted backgrounds, subtle structure |

**Usage rule:** Mostly light and airy. Forest Green as the emotional anchor. Deep Ocean for readable text. Ocean Teal used sparingly for freshness. Avoid high saturation everywhere.

---

### Typography

**Heading Font:** Playfair Display  
- Use for: H1, H2, H3, hero sections, emotional statements, cinematic moments  
- Weights: 600, 700  
- Feel: elegant, cinematic, warm, timeless

**Body Font:** Inter  
- Use for: paragraphs, buttons, cards, labels, navigation, forms  
- Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700  
- Feel: clean, readable, calm, modern

**Accent Script:** Great Vibes  
- Use ONLY sparingly: underlines, emphasis words, emotional accents  
- NEVER overuse

#### Type Scale

| Level | Font | Size (Desktop) | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Playfair Display | 64–84px | 700 |
| H2 | Playfair Display | 44–60px | 700 |
| H3 | Playfair Display | 32–40px | 600 |
| H4 | Inter | 24–32px | 600 |
| H5 | Inter | 20–28px | 500 |
| H6 | Inter | 13–15px (caps) | 700 |
| Paragraph | Inter | 17–19px | 400 |

Line height for body: 1.65–1.8. Letter spacing on H1: slightly negative.

---

### Spacing

- **Everything must breathe.** Generous padding and margins throughout.
- Large section gaps (120px–200px)
- Nothing cramped, nothing edge-to-edge
- Page should feel airy, cinematic, immersive

---

### Buttons

- **Style:** Pill shape (border-radius: 999px)
- **Primary:** Background `#1F4D3A`, text `#FAF8F4`, Inter SemiBold
- **Hover:** Background `#2F684F`, slight lift
- **Padding:** Generous (16px 40px minimum)
- Should feel touchable, premium, calm — NOT aggressive startup CTAs

---

### Cards

- Large border radius (20–32px)
- Soft green-tinted shadows (not harsh black)
- Warm background (Warm Sand or Light Cream)
- Generous padding
- Should feel like calm containers, not sharp widgets

---

### Animations

- Soft fades on scroll
- Subtle lifts on hover
- Smooth transitions
- NO bouncing, spinning, aggressive motion

## Copy & Tone Guidelines

**The writing should feel:**
- Conversational and emotionally grounded
- Founder-focused, not company-focused
- Clear, intelligent, natural
- Slightly imperfect — human, not AI-polished
- Like a founder who deeply understands other founders

**Avoid:**
- Corporate jargon
- Startup clichés
- Fake urgency or scarcity
- Internet marketer patterns
- Robotic or overly polished AI copy

**The core test:**  
If a founder reads this and thinks *"I finally feel understood"* — the copy is working.
